HOBS Companion — Home of Beautiful Souls Foundation
Last updated: October 16, 2026

Home of Beautiful Souls Foundation ("HOBS," "we," "us," or "our") operates the HOBS Companion mobile application (the "App"), a mental health support tool. This policy explains what information we collect through the App, why we collect it, how it's stored and protected, and the choices you have.

We take the sensitivity of this information seriously. HOBS exists to support people through difficult moments, and we hold the data you share with that same care.

2.3 Google Calendar integration

HOBS Companion offers mental health professionals on our platform the option to connect their own Google Calendar to the App.

What we access. If a professional chooses to connect their calendar, we request permission to view and create calendar events on that professional's own Google Calendar. We do not access any other Google service (Gmail, Drive, Contacts, etc.), and we only ever access the calendar belonging to the professional who explicitly connects it — never a client's calendar, and never any calendar the professional hasn't personally authorized.

Why we access it. This connection is used solely to:

  • Automatically create a calendar event (with a Google Meet video link) when a client books a session with that professional.
  • Update or remove that event if the session is rescheduled or cancelled within HOBS Companion.
  • Reflect changes made directly in Google Calendar back into HOBS Companion, so both stay in sync.
  • Recognize when a professional has personal time blocked on their calendar, so that time is not offered to clients as available for booking.

What we store. We store the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain this connection, encrypted at rest, along with the Google account email associated with the connection. We do not store the content of any calendar event that isn't related to a HOBS Companion session — for example, we do not read or retain details of a professional's personal appointments beyond knowing that a time slot is unavailable.

How a professional can revoke access. A professional can disconnect their Google Calendar at any time from within the App (Profile/Schedule → Calendar → Disconnect), which immediately deletes the stored tokens. They can also revoke access directly from their Google Account's security settings at any time, independent of the App.

Data sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share Google Calendar data with any third party. It is used exclusively for the scheduling functionality described above.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, our database and backend provider, on cloud infrastructure located in Japan. Push notifications and usage analytics are processed through Google Firebase, which operates on Google's global infrastructure. If you write a journal entry, its text is also sent to Groq, an AI inference provider, for the sole purpose of the second-layer crisis-detection check described in Section 5 below — Groq's policy is that this data is not retained for training and is not used to improve their models.

Access to your data within our systems is restricted using row-level security — in plain terms, our database is configured so that, by default, each person can only read their own data, and only designated admin or therapist accounts (for clients under their active care) can see more, and only what's relevant to that care relationship.

4. Who we share information with

  • Your therapist, if you book a session — limited to what's relevant to your care (e.g. shared journal entries you've explicitly marked as shared, session notes, booking history). Your therapist does not see mood or journal entries you haven't chosen to share. The only exception is your safety: if the app finds signs that you may be at risk, your team is told so they can reach out and support you. What they see is limited, and is set out in "When you might be struggling".
  • Google/Firebase, for delivering push notifications and for aggregated usage analytics.
  • Groq, an AI inference provider, for the second-layer crisis-detection check on journal entries described in Section 5, and as a voice-to-text backup. Groq does not retain this data for training or to improve their models.
  • Google, also for HOBS's email, video sessions (Meet), session recordings, transcripts and notes (Gemini), and professionals' calendars.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), for messages, including SOS alerts and phone-number checks.
  • Gladia, to turn voice notes into text. On HOBS's paid plan it deletes the audio and text after 3 weeks.
  • Razorpay, for payments (India).
  • HubSpot, for your name, email and screening test results (score, level, and any self-harm flag), so HOBS's team can follow up (USA).
  • Your emergency contacts, only when you press SOS: your alert, live location, phone number and address.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not use your mental health data for any purpose other than providing and improving the App and your care.

5. Sensitive data — extra care

Some of what HOBS Companion collects is especially sensitive: journal entries, mood patterns, and screening responses that may indicate distress, including self-harm risk. We want to be direct about how this is handled:

  • This data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.
  • It is not shared with employers, insurers, or any third party without your explicit action to share it (e.g. choosing to share an entry with your therapist).
  • Journal entries are checked for possible crisis signals using two layers: an instant on-device keyword check, and a second check that sends the entry's text to Groq (an AI provider) for a more careful read of indirect or metaphorical language a keyword check alone can miss. If either layer detects a possible signal, Bob checks in with you and your team may be told, as described in "When you might be struggling" below.

When you might be struggling

Your words are private. Your journal, your chats with Bob and what you write in the app are not shared with anyone just because you wrote them. There is one exception, and it exists to keep you safe.

Wherever you write in the app, it checks for signs that you may be struggling. If it finds them, Bob asks how you are and gives you choices. Your team (the professionals you are connected with and HOBS's admin) may also be told, so that someone who cares about you can check in. This is what they receive:

  • Signs of distress: a private note that you may be struggling. Never your words.
  • Signs of serious risk: what you answered, and your own words if you choose to type them.
  • Bob chat: if a message to Bob shows serious worry, your therapist and HOBS's admin see that message, so they can reach you quickly.
  • SOS: when you press SOS, your team and your emergency contacts get your alert and your live location until you are safe.

Everyone on your team is bound by confidentiality. They use this only to care for you, never for anything else, and it is never shared outside your care.

Help is always shown on screen: 112, Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, Government of India), and iCall 9152987821 (Mon–Sat, 8am–9pm).

HOBS Companion is not an emergency or crisis service. If you are in immediate danger or crisis, please call 112 (India's emergency number), Tele-MANAS at 14416 (free, Government of India), or iCall at 9152987821 (Mon–Sat, 8am–9pm). The App is a support tool, not a substitute for emergency care.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and edit most of your profile information directly in the App (Profile → Edit Profile).
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated data yourself, immediately, either in the App (Profile → Edit Profile → Delete My Account) or from the web without needing the App installed at delete-account.html. This is permanent and cannot be undone. A small number of records (e.g. confirmed payment/donation records) are retained for legal and accounting purposes, with identifying details removed. Session recordings, transcripts and notes are kept as described in "Data retention" below. Staff accounts (therapists, admins) can't be self-deleted this way since it affects real clients — contact us at info@homeofbeautifulsouls.com instead.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time from your device settings or in-app preferences.
  • Sharing with your therapist: you decide which journal entries your therapist sees. The only exception is your safety: if the app finds signs that you may be at risk, your team is told so they can reach out and support you. "When you might be struggling" sets out exactly what they see.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Someone to act for you: you can name someone to act for you if you cannot.
  • Complaints: you can complain to HOBS at info@homeofbeautifulsouls.com, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except:

  • payment and billing records, kept as Indian tax and accounting law requires;
  • session recordings, transcripts and notes, kept for 3 years after you stop using HOBS's services, or, if you were under 18, until you turn 21 if that is later, and then deleted.

8. Children's privacy

HOBS Companion is for people aged 16 and over. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or legal guardian to sign the Client Agreement with them, and payments for them are made by the parent or guardian outside the app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, please contact us at info@homeofbeautifulsouls.com so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, database-level access controls, and restricted admin access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously given the sensitivity of what we hold.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above, and the app will show you what changed and ask you to agree again before you continue.
